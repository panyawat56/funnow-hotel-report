# Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Hotel Report Demo is a personal demo for a job application. It is not a public service, and only its builder uses it.

## What it accesses

When its builder connects a dedicated demo Google account, the app can:

- read the demo Google Sheet that holds mock hotel data
- send test emails from that demo account

## How data is used

- Mock hotel names, revenue and order counts are used to create test summaries.
- Those mock figures may be sent to an AI model provider to draft the text.
- Test emails go only to addresses controlled by the builder.
- Google sign-in tokens stay in the builder's local n8n installation.

## What it does not do

- It does not collect data from the public.
- It does not sell or share Google account data for advertising.
- It does not use real hotel or customer data.
